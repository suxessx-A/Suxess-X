Update the "Conflict with Manager" coaching flow to align with the upgraded negotiation framework.

DO NOT remove the current structure.
Enhance it with stronger clarity, positioning, and response handling.

Make the following changes:

---

1. ADD NEW SECTION ABOVE "CHALLENGER SCRIPT"

Title: "INTERNAL CLARITY (BEFORE YOU SPEAK)"

Content:

Before entering the conversation, define:

- What do I actually want to change? (be specific)
- What impact is this having on my work, results, or energy?
- What is my boundary if this continues?

Guidance:
Do not enter the conversation just to express frustration.
Enter to create a shift.

---

2. UPDATE STEP 2 "OPEN THE CONVERSATION"

Replace with:

"I want to talk about something that’s affecting how I’m able to do my best work. Is now a good time?"

Keep it calm, neutral, and grounded.
Remove overly soft or vague phrasing.

---

3. UPDATE STEP 3 "BUILD AGREEMENT"

Replace with:

"I’ve noticed [specific behavior]. I want to make sure we’re aligned because it’s starting to impact [specific outcome]. How do you see it?"

Goal:
- Stay factual
- Invite their perspective
- Do NOT accuse

---

4. UPDATE STEP 4 "STATE THE IMPACT"

Tighten to:

"The impact of this is [clear business or performance impact]. I want to make sure we’re set up to succeed here."

Remove emotional over-explanation.

---

5. UPDATE STEP 5 "MAKE THE ASK"

Replace with:

"What I need going forward is [clear change]. Can we agree on that?"

Add instruction:
Pause. Let them respond first.

---

6. ADD NEW SECTION AFTER STEP 5

Title: "HANDLE PUSHBACK"

Add 3 common responses:

If they get defensive:
"I hear that. That’s not my intention — I want us to work better together. How do we move forward from here?"

If they minimize:
"I understand it may not seem significant, but it is impacting my ability to deliver at my best."

If they deflect:
"That may be true as well. For now, I’d like to stay focused on this specific point."

---

7. ADD MICRO-COACHING NOTE

Title: "DISCIPLINE"

Content:

- Do not over-explain
- Do not fill silence
- Do not rescue the conversation

Silence creates pressure. Let it work for you.

---

8. KEEP TONE CONSISTENT WITH NEGOTIATION FLOW

- Direct
- Calm
- Outcome-focused
- No corporate fluff
- No therapy-style language

---

Goal:
Make the conflict flow feel like a confident, controlled conversation — not an emotional discussion.
