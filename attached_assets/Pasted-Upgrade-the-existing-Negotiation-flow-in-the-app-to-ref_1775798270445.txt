Upgrade the existing "Negotiation" flow in the app to reflect executive-level coaching, not generic advice.

Do NOT reference any frameworks or authors explicitly. Embed the thinking and structure directly.

The flow must guide the user through:
1. Clarifying their position (what they want, their value, walk-away)
2. Structuring the conversation
3. Handling pushback (especially "no budget")
4. Taking action with confidence

---

SECTION 1: INTERNAL ALIGNMENT (before the conversation)

Title: "Clarify your position before you speak"

Add guided steps:

1. What you want (target outcome)
- Prompt user to define a specific number or range
- Not vague (e.g. not “more” or “higher”)
- Example:
  “Define your target range based on your role, impact, and market”

2. Your value (evidence, not opinion)
- Prompt user to list:
  - Measurable results (impact, revenue, efficiency, outcomes)
  - Scope of responsibility
  - Any market benchmarks
- Instruction:
  “If you can’t prove it, you can’t negotiate it”

3. Your walk-away point
- Prompt user to define:
  - Minimum acceptable outcome
  - What they will do if not met (stay, push later, explore options)
- Instruction:
  “Power comes from knowing what you will and won’t accept before the conversation”

---

SECTION 2: HOW TO LEAD THE CONVERSATION

Title: "Lead with impact, not emotion"

Replace any existing generic scripts with:

Structure:

1. Frame:
“My role and responsibilities have evolved, and I’d like to talk about how my compensation reflects the impact I’m having.”

2. Anchor value:
“I’ve taken on [specific results / responsibilities].”

3. Align:
“I’d like to make sure my compensation reflects that.”

4. Ask:
“Can we review this together?”

5. Pause:
- Explicit instruction: “Stop talking. Let them respond.”

---

SECTION 3: USING MARKET DATA (OPTIONAL)

Title: "Use data without losing control"

Add guidance:

- Use market data to support your position, not complain
- Avoid comparing yourself to specific individuals

Example phrasing:
“Based on market benchmarks and my current scope, I’d like to review how my compensation aligns.”

---

SECTION 4: HANDLING PUSHBACK (CRITICAL)

Title: "Stay in control when they push back"

Scenario: Manager says “We don’t have budget”

Teach this response structure:

1. Acknowledge (calm, no resistance):
“It sounds like budget is tight right now.”

2. Re-anchor value:
“Given the scope and impact I’m delivering…”

3. Ask a calibrated question:
“What would need to happen for this to be possible?”

4. Lock next step:
“Can we set a time to revisit this?”

---

Add additional tools:

- Use short reflective statements to prompt them to expand:
  Example:
  Manager: “There’s no flexibility”
  User: “No flexibility?”
  (Then pause)

- Encourage silence after key questions:
  “Let them fill the space. Do not rush to speak.”

---

SECTION 5: IF THEY DELAY OR STALL

Add:

If manager says:
“Let’s revisit later”

User should respond:

“What specific outcomes or milestones would you want to see to support that?”

Goal:
Turn vague delay into clear criteria.

---

SECTION 6: ALTERNATIVE PATH (IF COMPENSATION BLOCKED)

Add:

“If compensation isn’t flexible right now, explore alternatives:
- Title change
- Expanded scope
- Defined timeline for review”

Example:
“If compensation isn’t flexible right now, are there other ways we can reflect this or set a clear review point?”

---

SECTION 7: ACT NOW

Update action steps:

1. Define your target compensation range (specific numbers)
2. Write 3–5 measurable proof points of your value
3. Set your walk-away point (non-negotiable)
4. Prepare your opening line and practice it out loud
5. Schedule the conversation within 48 hours

---

SECTION 8: IDENTITY ANCHOR

Replace existing identity line with:

“You know your value, you communicate it clearly, and you don’t leave conversations without a next step.”

- Direct, calm, authoritative
- No corporate jargon
- No motivational fluff
- Feels like real executive coaching
- Practical and immediately usable
