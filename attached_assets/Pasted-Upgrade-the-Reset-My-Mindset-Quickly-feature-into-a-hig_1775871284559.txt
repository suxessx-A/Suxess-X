Upgrade the "Reset My Mindset Quickly" feature into a high-performance cognitive coaching flow.

Current issue:
The experience is too shallow and action-driven. It lacks depth in identifying internal narratives, challenging thinking patterns, and creating real cognitive shifts.

Refactor ALL mindset reset flows using the following 8-step structure:

1. TRIGGER AWARENESS

* Clearly name the emotional state and situation
* Example: "You received critical feedback and now feel doubt and pressure"

2. PATTERN INTERRUPTION (NEW)

* Call out what is happening psychologically
* Examples:

  * "You are treating feedback as a verdict, not data"
  * "Your mind is turning one moment into a full identity judgment"
* Goal: create awareness and distance from the reaction

3. STORY IDENTIFICATION

* Surface the internal narrative driving the emotion
* Examples:

  * "This means I'm not good enough"
  * "I'm failing"
* Make the story explicit and concise

4. TRUTH VS DISTORTION

* Separate facts from interpretation
* Format:

  * Fact: what actually happened
  * Interpretation: the meaning the user assigned
* Keep sharp and minimal

5. REFRAME

* Replace the limiting belief with a grounded, empowering alternative
* Examples:

  * "This is feedback, not a verdict"
  * "This is direction for improvement"
* Avoid motivational or vague language

6. POWER QUESTIONS (CRITICAL)

* Add 2–3 short, high-impact questions to force cognitive shift
* Examples:

  * "What specifically is useful in this feedback?"
  * "What is actually in my control right now?"
  * "What would a top performer do next?"
* Questions must drive clarity and ownership

7. OWNERSHIP SHIFT

* Move user from passive → active (Passenger → Captain mindset)
* Include prompts like:

  * "What am I choosing to do next?"
  * "What outcome do I want to create from this?"
* Reinforce responsibility and control

8. PRECISION ACTION

* Provide 1–2 immediate, concrete actions
* Must be specific, relevant, and executable within minutes
* Examples:

  * Write down 2 key takeaways from feedback
  * Ask a clarifying question to your manager
  * Adjust one behavior in your next interaction

9. IDENTITY REINFORCEMENT

* Add a short, powerful belief statement
* Designed for repetition (22x mechanic)
* Examples:

  * "I use feedback to improve quickly"
  * "I take action despite uncertainty"
* Keep concise and credible

Apply this structure across all mindset scenarios:

* Fear of failure after feedback
* Feeling exposed or judged
* Mental spirals and overthinking
* Feeling undervalued or unseen

Tone:

* Direct, grounded, and authoritative
* Coaching-focused, not therapeutic
* Designed to create clarity, control, and forward movement

Goal:
The user should experience:
emotional reaction → awareness → cognitive shift → ownership → action → identity reinforcement

This must feel like interrupting unhelpful thinking and replacing it with intentional leadership thinking.
