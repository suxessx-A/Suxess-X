You are updating an executive coaching app that generates situational guidance across three strategic paths:

1. Challenge It Directly (already strong — do NOT change structure significantly)
2. Shift the Dynamic (needs full redesign)
3. Hold the Standard (needs full redesign)

Your task is to systematically rewrite ALL flows under:

* “Shift the Dynamic”
* “Hold the Standard”

---

## CORE PROBLEM TO FIX

Right now, all paths use the same structure:

* Diagnosis
* What to do
* Act now

This works for “Challenge Directly” (conversation-based), but fails for:

* Shift (influence strategy)
* Hold (control & positioning strategy)

The current outputs feel generic, repetitive, and action-based instead of strategic.

---

## OBJECTIVE

Transform “Shift” and “Hold” into DISTINCT EXECUTION MODELS.

Each path must feel like a different way to win the situation — not a variation of the same advice.

---

## NEW STRUCTURES TO IMPLEMENT

### 1. SHIFT THE DYNAMIC → Influence Model

Replace generic sections with:

#### STRATEGIC POSITIONING

* Who matters in this situation?
* Whose perception needs to shift?
* Where is the user currently invisible or misunderstood?

#### INFLUENCE MOVES

* What actions change perception WITHOUT direct confrontation?
* Pre-wiring conversations
* Creating visibility moments
* Aligning with stakeholders

#### VISIBILITY ACTIONS

* Specific ways to demonstrate value indirectly
* Where to speak, contribute, or show up differently
* Focus on leverage, not volume

#### GUIDING PRINCIPLE

Short, sharp line:
“I influence the outcome without forcing confrontation.”

---

### 2. HOLD THE STANDARD → Control Model

Replace generic sections with:

#### STANDARD DEFINITION

* What behavior, expectation, or boundary is not acceptable?
* Clarify this in objective, non-emotional terms

#### CONTROL MOVES

* Document facts and patterns
* Maintain performance without reacting emotionally
* Avoid premature escalation

#### TIMING DECISION

* When should the user act vs wait?
* What signals indicate it’s time to escalate or address directly?

#### GUIDING PRINCIPLE

Short, sharp line:
“I protect my position while maintaining control.”

---
